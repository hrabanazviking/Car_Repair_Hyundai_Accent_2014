# Quality-control exclusions from this research pass

These were **not** promoted despite some positive evidence because the public record contained a material caution:

- **Monro Auto Service & Tire Centers - 27th St, Milwaukee, WI:** transparent/no-upsell reviews coexist with a serious recent complaint involving service/payment confusion and a warning light after tire installation.
- **Forest Hill Garage - Richmond, VA:** good honesty comments, but an unresolved workmanship complaint involving a fuel-pump repair makes the current record too mixed.
- **Wilmington Tire & Auto - Wilmington, NC:** many honesty reviews, but a current customer described a quote as roughly double a prior-year price.
- **Twin Falls Chevrolet - Twin Falls, ID:** positive authorization comments coexist with a recent complaint that a costly diagnostic path missed a grounding-wire problem.
- **Aasby Automotive Service - Springfield, MO:** strong integrity comments but older evidence includes a specific complaint about a part charged well above retail.
- **GF United Auto Service - Freehold, NJ:** strong no-upsell reputation but a recent customer says filters were replaced without advance approval.

# Expansion 10 standouts

- **Husker Automotive - Lincoln, NE** - 5.0/42 and 25-year customer relationships.
- **East Side Service Center - Providence, RI** - explicitly refused to sell tires the customer did not need.
- **Redmond's Auto - Columbia, SC** - inspected a suspected brake problem and sold only a minor lubrication fix.
- **T.D.S. Auto Repair - Sioux Falls, SD** - tells customers what can safely wait until their budget improves.
- **Mike's Auto & Towing - Middlebury, VT** - 20-30 year customer relationships with repeated no-upsell evidence.
- **Price's Service Center - Blacksburg, VA** - 5.0/51 with strong honest-price and no-upsell reviews.

> As with every file in this directory, re-check the newest reviews before a major repair. Reputation drift is real.
