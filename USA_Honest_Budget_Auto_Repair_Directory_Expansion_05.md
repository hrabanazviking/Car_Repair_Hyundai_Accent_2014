# USA Honest, Helpful & Budget-Friendly Auto Repair Directory — Expansion 05

> **Scope:** Fifth major nationwide research expansion for all makes/models. This supplements the main directory plus Expansions 02, 03, and 04.
>
> **Research date:** 2026-09-13
>
> **New shops in this expansion:** 76 additional shops after removing exact-name duplicates found in the previous expansion files during this pass.
>
> **Selection rule:** High star ratings alone were not enough. Shops were favored when public or verified-service reviews specifically described fair pricing, honest diagnosis, refusal of unnecessary work, no-pressure recommendations, dealer-beating second opinions, repair prioritization, final bills below estimates, warranty referrals, or unusually helpful treatment of travelers and budget-conscious customers.

## Evidence grades

- **A+ — Exceptional:** unusually strong verified-review volume and/or detailed examples of a shop deliberately saving a customer from unnecessary expensive work.
- **A — Strong:** repeated honesty, fair-price, no-upsell, repair-priority or long-term-trust evidence.
- **B — Good candidate:** meaningful positive evidence but a smaller review sample, older evidence, narrower specialty or at least one reason to verify details carefully before major work.

> **Important:** Shops change. Ownership, technicians, prices, hours and workmanship can drift. Re-read the newest reviews and request a written estimate before authorizing major repairs. “Budget friendly” here means **good value and restrained recommendations**, not necessarily the lowest posted labor rate.

# Quality-control notes

- Exact-name duplicates that surfaced during this research but were already present in earlier expansion files were removed from this file.
- Candidates with meaningful current complaints about unnecessary work, serious misdiagnosis, unauthorized service or unexplained high pricing were excluded or not promoted.
- Some entries lack an address or phone in the captured search result. Rather than invent details, those fields are explicitly marked as not captured; use the linked source or current business listing before traveling.
- Verified CARFAX service reviews were favored because they tie comments to actual service events and often identify the serviced make/model.
- For repairs above roughly $1,000, major engine/transmission work, hybrid/EV battery work, diesel injection work or advanced electrical diagnosis, obtain a second written opinion whenever practical.

# Traveler call script

Before authorizing work, ask:

1. “What is the diagnostic fee, and is it credited toward the repair?”
2. “Please separate parts, labor, taxes and shop fees on the estimate.”
3. “What must be repaired now for safety or reliability, and what can wait?”
4. “Is any of this potentially covered by a manufacturer warranty, recall, extended warranty or parts warranty?”
5. “Will you call me before exceeding the written estimate or adding any work?”
6. “What parts-and-labor warranty comes with this repair, and is it honored nationwide?”

The strongest signal in this directory remains **restraint**: a shop that tells a customer *not* to buy a repair has demonstrated more than a five-star average ever can.
