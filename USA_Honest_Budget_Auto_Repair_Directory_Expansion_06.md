# USA Honest, Helpful & Budget-Friendly Auto Repair Directory — Expansion 06

> **Scope:** Sixth major nationwide research expansion for all makes/models. This supplements the main directory plus Expansions 02–05.
>
> **Research date:** 2026-09-13
>
> **New shops in this expansion:** 70+ additional shops after removing known exact-name duplicates from prior files.
>
> **Selection rule:** A high star average alone was not enough. Shops were favored when public or verified-service reviews specifically described fair pricing, honest diagnosis, refusal of unnecessary work, no-pressure recommendations, cheaper second opinions, repair prioritization, warranty awareness, bills below estimates, or unusually helpful treatment of travelers and budget-conscious customers.

## Evidence grades

- **A+ — Exceptional:** unusually strong verified-review volume and/or detailed examples of a shop deliberately saving a customer from unnecessary expense.
- **A — Strong:** repeated honesty, fair-price, no-upsell, repair-priority or long-term-trust evidence.
- **B — Good candidate:** meaningful positive evidence but a smaller sample, older evidence or narrower specialty.

> **Important:** Ownership, staff, prices and workmanship can change. Re-read the newest reviews and request a written estimate before major work. “Budget friendly” means strong value and restrained recommendations, not necessarily the lowest hourly rate.

# Research and quality-control notes

- Known exact-name duplicates from the prior directory files were removed during this pass.
- Shops with meaningful recent allegations of unauthorized work, severe misdiagnosis, repeated unexplained overpricing or serious workmanship failures were not promoted merely because their aggregate rating was high.
- Verified CARFAX service reviews were heavily favored because comments are associated with actual service events and often identify the serviced vehicle.
- A small review sample can still earn an **A** when the behavior described is unusually concrete, but large repairs should always be independently re-checked.
- For repairs over roughly $1,000, major engine/transmission work, hybrid/EV systems, diesel injection or difficult electrical diagnosis, obtain a second written opinion whenever practical.
- The strongest indicator remains **restraint**: the shop that tells a customer not to spend money has provided much stronger evidence than a generic five-star compliment.
