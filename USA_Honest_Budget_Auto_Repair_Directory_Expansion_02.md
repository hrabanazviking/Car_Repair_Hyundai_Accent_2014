# USA Honest, Helpful & Budget-Friendly Auto Repair Directory — Expansion 02

> **Scope:** Additional U.S. repair shops for all makes/models, researched as a supplement to `USA_Honest_Budget_Auto_Repair_Directory.md`.
> **Research date:** 2026-09-13
> **New shops in this expansion:** 77
> **Selection rule:** High star ratings alone were not enough. Preference was given to recent public/verified reviews specifically describing fair prices, no unnecessary upselling, honest diagnosis, cheaper alternatives, accurate estimates, repair prioritization, long-term trust, or unusually helpful treatment of stranded/budget-conscious customers.

## Evidence grades

- **A+ — Exceptional:** unusually large verified-review footprint and/or extraordinary documented honesty/value examples.
- **A — Strong:** repeated recent honesty/value/no-upsell evidence from public or verified reviews.
- **B — Good candidate:** strong positive evidence but a smaller sample or less complete service data.

> **Important:** Shops, staff, prices, ownership, hours and quality can change. Re-check the newest reviews and obtain a written estimate before authorizing major work. This supplement deliberately excludes several shops found during research where recent reviews raised material upsell, pricing or workmanship concerns.

# Research notes

- Verified-service sources such as CARFAX were heavily favored in this expansion because they make it easier to separate real service experiences from generic reputation marketing.
- A high rating alone did not qualify a shop. The review text needed to contain meaningful evidence of honesty, value, restraint in recommendations, budget awareness or unusually helpful service.
- Several superficially attractive candidates were deliberately omitted because current reviews included serious complaints about upselling, excessive pricing, inaccurate diagnosis or workmanship.
- “Budget friendly” does **not** necessarily mean “cheapest hourly rate.” A shop that correctly diagnoses a $200 problem instead of selling a $2,000 parts cannon is often the truly cheaper shop.
- For expensive engine, transmission, hybrid/EV battery, diesel-injection or European-specialty work, still obtain a second written opinion whenever practical.
